## Why ProStore?

- No PC after setup!
- Unlimited app installs!
- Easy certificate management!
- App store or upload .ipa

|  | ProStore | SideStore | AltStore | Esign |
|:-----------|:--------:|:---------:|:--------:|:-----:|
| **No PC for Setup**   | ⏰       | ❌        | ❌       | ✅    |
| **No PC for Every App Install**      | ✅       | ✅        | ❌       | ✅    |
| **Direct Install**      | ✅       | ✅        | ✅       | ❌    |
| **itms-services Install**      | ⏰       | ❌        | ❌       | ✅    |
| **Unlimited Apps**      | ✅       | ❌        | ❌       | ✅    |
| **Custom IPA**      | ⏰       | ✅        | ✅       | ✅    |
| **App Store**      | ✅       | 🟡        | 🟡       | 🟡    |
